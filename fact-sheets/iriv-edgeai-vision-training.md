# Product fact sheets

## Product name
IRIV EdgeAI Industry 4.0 Training: Getting Started with EdgeAI Vision

## Aliases
V-KIT-IRIV-EAI, KIT-IRIV-EAI-SP, IRIV EdgeAI Industry 4.0 Training, IRIV EdgeAI Industry 4.0 Workshop, IRIV EdgeAI Workshop, IRIV EdgeAI Industry 4.0 Complete Training Kit, IRIV EdgeAI Training Kit, IRIV Edge AI Training

## What it is
A Cytron two-day Edge AI vision workshop sold with an IRIV EdgeAI training kit that participants take home. The kit is a DIN-rail training box with an IRIV EdgeAI CM5 controller (8GB RAM, 32GB eMMC, 13 TOPS Hailo AI accelerator), RS485 sensors, a relay, an illuminated push button and a USB camera, and the course runs from Node-RED and Modbus basics to training and running a custom AI vision model.

## Interfaces and compatibility
- Controller documented separately in the IRIV EdgeAI CM5 file: 2 isolated DI, 2 isolated dry-contact DO, RS485 on /dev/ttyACM0, 2x USB 3.0 on one shared hub, 2x MIPI CSI, M.2 NVMe only, mPCIe USB 2.0 only (controller datasheet)
- Up to four cameras: two USB 3.0 plus two MIPI CSI ports (product page)
- Power meter and temperature/humidity sensor use RS485 Modbus (product page)
- Requires a Windows laptop (product page)
