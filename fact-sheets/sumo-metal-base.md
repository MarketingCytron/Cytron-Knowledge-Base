Metal Base for Sumo Robot

## Aliases

## What it is
A flat black metal plate (8.3 x 5 x 0.3 cm, 96 g) with M3 mounting holes, screwed into sumo and soccer robot bodies to add weight. Sold as a replacement part or an upgrade.
