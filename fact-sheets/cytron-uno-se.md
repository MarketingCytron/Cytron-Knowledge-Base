# Product fact sheets

## Product name
Cytron UNO Special Edition - Arduino Compatible

## Aliases
CT-UNO-SE, Cytron UNO Special Edition, Cytron UNO SE, Cytron UNO Special Edition - Arduino Compatible

## What it is
Cytron UNO (CT-UNO) with special side connectors that let it be stacked on top of, or at the bottom of, a shield. The product page says it is "exactly the same as CT-UNO, with the special side connectors": an Arduino UNO R3-compatible board with an SMD ATmega328P (Optiboot), FTDI FT231X USB-serial on Micro-B and a 7–15 V DC jack.

## Specs and functions
- Product code: CT-UNO-SE
- Warranty: 12 months (product page)
- Special feature: special side connectors to stack the board on top of or at the bottom of a shield; otherwise exactly the same as CT-UNO (product page)
- Documentation: CT-UNO User's Manual (V1.0, Nov 2014), CT-UNO Schematic, CT-UNO Fritzing file (linked from the product page)
- Microcontroller: SMD ATmega328 (ATmega328p in spec section) with Optiboot (UNO) bootloader; 32KB Flash, 2KB SRAM, 1KB EEPROM; 16MHz
- USB-to-serial: FTDI FT231X; USB Micro-B socket
- DC input: Adapter Socket (2.1mm); 7–15 V recommended, 6–20 V limits
- 5V regulator: 1 A max (with Vin at 12V); 3.3V regulator: 500 mA max
- I/O: 14 digital I/O (6 PWM), 6 analog inputs; 0-5V digital outputs with 3.3V compatible inputs
- LEDs: RX, TX, Power (green), programmable LED on pin 13 (active HIGH); reset button; ISP 6-pin header; extra pads; white PCB
- Dimensions: 68.58 mm × 55.88 mm; 4 × Ø3 mm mounting holes (CT-UNO-SE Dimensions drawing)
- Packing list: [NOT STATED] in text; product photo shows the board with four separate stacking headers

## Interfaces and compatibility
- Arduino R3 standard headers, R3 shield compatible; side connectors let it sit on top of or under a shield
- Arduino IDE board "Arduino Uno"; FTDI VCP driver for the FT231X

## Known limitations
- Same limits as CT-UNO: 5V regulator 1 A rating given with Vin at 12V; 3.3V regulator limited to 500 mA; adapter input 7–15 V recommended, 6–20 V limits; FTDI VCP driver needed
- USB Micro-B socket only (no USB-C)
