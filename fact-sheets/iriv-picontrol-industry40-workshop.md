## What it is
A Cytron two-day Industry 4.0 workshop built around Cytron's IRIV PiControl CM4 industrial controller, sold with an IRIV PiControl Industry 4.0 Training Kit that participants keep. It runs at Cytron HQ in Bukit Mertajam, Penang (max 5 participants) or at the customer's company (min 3 participants), and the kit is also sold on its own.

## Interfaces and compatibility
- Controller documented separately in the IRIV PiControl CM4 file: RS485 on /dev/ttyACM0, RS232 on /dev/ttyACM1, 4 isolated DI, 4 isolated DO, 4 isolated AI, mini PCIe USB 2.0 and SPI only, M.2 Key-M NVMe only (controller datasheet)
- Temperature and humidity sensor and ADL200 power meter both use RS485 Modbus RTU (product page)
- Software used: Node-RED and dashboard, MQTT, database, Blynk, ThingSpeak, Python, Linux CLI (product page)
- Optional controller add-ons (NVMe SSD, 4G/LoRa mini PCIe) are not included and are by enquiry (product page)
