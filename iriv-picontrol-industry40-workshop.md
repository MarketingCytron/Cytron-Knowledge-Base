## What it is
A Cytron hands-on Industry 4.0 workshop built around Cytron's own IRIV PiControl CM4 industrial controller, sold together with an IRIV PiControl Industry 4.0 Training Kit that participants take home [PP]. The kit is a DIN-rail panel with the controller, a 24V DIN-rail PSU, an illuminated push button, an RS485 temperature and humidity sensor and an ADL200 single-phase AC power meter (RS485 Modbus RTU) [PP]. The workshop runs 9.00 am – 5.00 pm, two days, either at Cytron Technologies HQ in Bukit Mertajam, Penang (max 5 participants per workshop) or at the customer's company (min 3 participants) [PP]. It covers IR4.0 and IRIV PiControl hardware, Node-RED and its dashboard, RS485 Modbus sensors and the power meter, MQTT, data gathering and a database, Blynk and ThingSpeak IoT platforms, basic Python and the Linux command line [PP]. Cytron issues a participation certificate and states the course is HRDC claimable [PP]. The same page also sells the training kit alone ("Training Kit ONLY") [PP].

## Interfaces, terminals and pin mapping
Not applicable as a service. Kit wiring is not documented on the page or brochures. Controller facts that matter for the exercises (from `iriv-picontrol-cm4.md`): RS485 is `/dev/ttyACM0` and RS232 is `/dev/ttyACM1` (USB-to-dual-UART chip, not the CM4 GPIO UART); 4 isolated DI, 4 isolated DO, 4 isolated AI; mini PCIe = USB 2.0 and SPI only (no PCIe); M.2 Key-M = NVMe only. Both the temperature/humidity sensor and the ADL200 power meter are RS485 Modbus RTU devices on the same bus [PP], so they need different Modbus addresses (inferred; addresses and baud rates are not stated).

## Rules and gotchas (for code, wiring and tutorials)
- **Name the controller variant:** the kit's controller is IRIV PiControl **CM4**, Wireless **2GB RAM / 16GB eMMC** [PP]. Never write bare "IRIV PiControl" in content about this kit as if it could be the CM5 (`IRIV-PICL5`).
- **Duration:** the description, option names, brochures and landing page say **two days**; the packing list (and the on-site option page title) still say "one-day" / "1 day on-site Training" [PP]. Use two days and flag the old wording.
- **Fee vs cart price:** the description's RM3500 per seat is not what the HQ option pages charge (RM4,265.99 Slot 1; RM3,675.00 Slots 2–3) [PP]. Quote the option page price with its read date, or "RM3500 per seat (page description)" — not both as one figure.
- **On-site minimum:** this page says 3 pax minimum [PP][UM]; the generic landing-page FAQ says on-site corporate training is for teams of 8 or more [PP: landing]. The product page is specific to this workshop — use 3 pax and flag the FAQ.
- **Slot 3 dates** are printed as "25 - 27 February 2026" (three dates for a two-day workshop) [PP] — likely a typo; do not repeat.
- **Brochure dates are stale:** the linked brochures print Jul/Aug/Sep 2025 dates, not the Dec 2025 – Feb 2026 slots they are linked from [UM][PP].
- The trainer laptop requirement is Windows or macOS [PP] (the EdgeAI training requires Windows — do not mix the two).
- Never copy the contact phone/email printed on the brochures, or default controller logins.
