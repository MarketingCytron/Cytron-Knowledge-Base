## What it is
- Cytron OEM wheels for sumo robots: a light-blue **silicone tyre on an aluminium rim**, sold as a set, rim only or tyre only [PP].
- **42 × 30 mm** for **1 kg sumo robots**; **32 × 22 mm** for **500 g sumo robots** [PP].
- "The wheels come with individual items, not in pairs. Users need to buy 2 units if they want the wheels in pairs." (both pages) [PP].
- The 42 × 30 mm page: "The wheels didn't come with M2 Allen Key; please buy it separately if needed." Both pages offer the M2 Allen Key (RM0.90) as an accessory [PP].
- The 42 × 30 mm page says the wheels are "trusted by SUMO:BIT Robot Kits for micro:bit" [PP] (see `sumobit-robot-kit.md`).
- Warranty 12 months (both pages) [PP].

## Interfaces, terminals and pin mapping
- Mechanical part; no electrical interface [PP].
- **Shaft fit:** 42 × 30 mm → 4 mm shaft; 32 × 22 mm → 3 mm shaft [PP]. The 42 × 30 mm rim is held by **two M4 set screws** in threaded holes [PP: drawing]; the SUMO:BIT guide also fixes each rim "by two screws on the motor shaft flat" (`sumobit-robot-kit.md`) [UM there].
- **Cup over the gearbox (42 × 30 mm):** the rim is a cup Ø26 mm × 21 mm deep with the hub at the outer face [PP: drawing]; with the page's "Motor Diameter: 25mm (centre shaft)" this means the front of a 25 mm (GM25) gearbox can sit inside the wheel — inferred from the drawing, not stated.
- **Assembly order (all linked tutorials):** fit the rim on the motor shaft and tighten it with the Allen key first, then slide or stretch the silicone tyre over the rim [PP: tutorial].

## Rules and gotchas (for code, wiring and tutorials)
- **Sold singly:** one wheel per unit; order 2 for a 2-wheel robot, 4 for a 4-wheel robot [PP].
- **Allen key not included** (42 × 30 mm page states it; 32 × 22 mm offers it as an accessory) [PP]. M4 set screws normally take a 2 mm hex key, which matches the "M2 Allen Key" offered (general practice, not stated by Cytron).
- **Two sizes are not interchangeable:** 4 mm vs 3 mm shaft, 1 kg vs 500 g class [PP]. Do not put the 42 × 30 mm wheel on a micro metal gearmotor or the 32 × 22 mm wheel on a GM25 motor.
- **Tyre and rim are separate parts:** "Silicone Only" has no rim; "Aluminium Rim Only" has no tyre [PP].
- **Set screws:** whether the M4 set screws come fitted in the rim is not stated (see *Needs verification*).
- **Hardness:** no Shore value is published; do not quote one.
- Weight counts toward the sumo class limit: 50 g per 42 × 30 mm wheel, about 21.5 g per 32 × 22 mm wheel [PP]; the 500 g tutorial reminds builders to weigh the finished robot [PP: tutorial].

## Common mistakes / troubleshooting
- **Received one wheel** → sold singly; buy 2 [PP].
- **Wheel slips on the shaft** → tighten both set screws onto the shaft flat with the Allen key before fitting the tyre [PP: drawing][PP: tutorial].
- **Rim won't go on the shaft** → wrong size: 42 × 30 mm needs a 4 mm shaft, 32 × 22 mm a 3 mm shaft [PP].
- **No Allen key in the bag** → sold separately (M2 Allen Key) [PP].
- **Tyre loses grip** → clean or wash the silicone (SUMO:BIT guide advice, `sumobit-robot-kit.md`).
