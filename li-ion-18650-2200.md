## What it is
A single cylindrical 18650-size lithium-ion cell, nominal 3.7 V (4.2 V when full), 2200 mAh, with a button-top (medium) positive terminal and flat negative terminal [PP]. It has NO built-in protection circuit and must be charged with a proper Li-ion charger with balancer [PP]. The product page stresses it is NOT an A/AA/AAA battery [PP]. The vendor specification identifies the cell as PKCELL ICR18650 2200mAh 3.7V [VEN].

### Product page facts (my.cytron.io, read 19 Oct 2026)
- **Page notes:** NOT an A/AA/AAA battery; battery warranty 1 week from purchase; cannot be shipped to Sabah, Sarawak or overseas [PP].
- **Use:** cells in series — 2 = 7.4 V nominal, 3 = 11.1 V nominal — with the matching holders; always charge with a proper Li-ion charger with balancer; no built-in protection circuit; check polarity before connecting [PP].
- **Recommended chargers (page links):** N2 Plus Universal Charger; Multifunction LiPo Balance Charger; 1A LiPo Charger Module with Protection [PP].
- **Holders (page links):** single 18650 holder (CN-BA-18650-01); two-cell series holder (CN-BA-18650-02); three-cell series holder (CN-BA-18650-03) [PP].
- **Spec list:** 3.7 V nominal (4.2 V full); minimum discharge 3.0 V; 2200 mAh; rapid charge 0.5C (~1.1 A), standard 0.2C (~440 mA); charge voltage 4.2 V ± 0.05 V; max discharge 1C (~2.2 A), standard 0.2C; size 2 × 6.5 × 2 cm; no protection circuit; medium button-top positive, flat negative; **life cycle > 500 times** under proper conditions [PP].
- **Packing list:** 1x 18650 2200 mAh battery [PP]. **Resources:** battery datasheet (Google Drive) [PP].

## Interfaces, terminals and pin mapping
- Two terminals only: positive = button top (medium height), negative = flat base [PP].
- No solder tabs, no wires, no connector, no protection PCB [PP].
- The product page links Cytron 18650 holders for 1, 2 and 3 cells in series (CN-BA-18650-01/-02/-03) [PP]. Other holders: check they accept button-top cells (6.5 cm stated [PP]; button-top cells are longer than flat-top cells).

## Rules and gotchas (for code, wiring and tutorials)
- **Unprotected cell.** "NO built-in protection circuit" [PP]. Any tutorial must put the cell behind a charger/protection board (BMS) or use a holder/board with protection; never connect it directly to a load without over-discharge protection.
- **Charge with a proper Li-ion charger with balancer** [PP]; charging voltage 4.2 V ± 0.05 V [PP]; vendor charge method is CC 0.5C / CV 4.2 V, 0.01C cut-off [VEN].
- **Discharge limits differ by source.** Product page: max 1C (~2.2 A) [PP]; vendor: 3300 mA (1.5C) continuous [VEN]. The product page outranks the vendor sheet — state 1C / 2.2 A as the limit in content.
- **Charge-rate limits differ by source.** Product page "rapid charge 0.5C ~1.1A" [PP]; vendor "maximum constant charging current 2200mA (1.0C)" [VEN]. State 0.5C / 1.1 A as the rapid-charge figure.
- **Cut-off at 3.0 V** [PP][VEN]; do not discharge below it.
- **Charge temperature 0–45 °C; discharge -20–60 °C** [VEN] (no Cytron figure).
- **NOT an A/AA/AAA battery** [PP] — 18650 is 18 mm diameter, ~65 mm long; it does not fit AA holders.
- **Shipping**: Cytron cannot ship it to Sabah, Sarawak or overseas [PP]. Mention this in any article aimed at East Malaysia or international readers.
- **Warranty is 1 week** on the battery [PP] (not the usual 12 months).
- Vendor label warnings (reproduce in safety boxes): use a specified charger; do not throw into fire or heat; do not short-circuit the terminals; do not disassemble; do not reverse polarity; do not connect to a mains outlet; do not solder directly to the cell or pierce it; do not strike or throw it; keep away from metal objects (necklaces, hairpins) in transport/storage; do not immerse in water; if it smells, heats, discolours or deforms, remove it to a contained metal box; tape terminals before disposal [VEN].
- Vendor liability note: the manufacturer is not liable for overheating, fire or explosion caused by mishandling [VEN].

## Power and electrical limits
- Voltage: 3.0 V (discharge cut-off) to 4.2 V (charge cut-off); nominal 3.7 V [PP][VEN].
- Capacity: 2200 mAh typical; 2090 mAh minimum [VEN]; 2200 mAh [PP].
- Charge: standard 0.2C ~440 mA, rapid 0.5C ~1.1 A [PP]; vendor max 1.0C 2200 mA [VEN].
- Discharge: standard 0.2C ~440 mA, max 1C ~2.2 A [PP]; vendor max continuous 1.5C 3300 mA [VEN].
- Vendor safety tests passed (no fire, no explosion, no leakage): overcharge (charge to 4.45 V at 3C), overdischarge (30 Ω for 24 h), vibration, drop (1 m onto concrete, twice), collision, crush (about 13 kN for 30 min), short circuit (≤ 50 mΩ; surface ≤ 150 °C), hot oven 130 ± 2 °C 10 min, thermal shock, impact (10 kg weight, 56 mm bar, 1 m) [VEN]. These are vendor type-test statements, not a licence to abuse the cell.

## Common mistakes / troubleshooting
- Cell swells, heats or dies early → over-discharged below 3.0 V or charged above 4.2 V; it has no protection circuit [PP][VEN].
- Does not fit a holder → it is a button-top 18650, 6.5 cm long [PP]; many holders are sized for flat-top cells.
- Order refused for East Malaysia / overseas → shipping restriction [PP].
- Expecting 12-month warranty → battery warranty is 1 week [PP].
- Pulling > 2.2 A → above the product page's 1C limit [PP].
- Charging in a cold room → vendor charge range starts at 0 °C [VEN].
